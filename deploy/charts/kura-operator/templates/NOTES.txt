{{ include "weebo-operator.notes" . }}

1. Switch kura-server to the operator (the `kura` chart's values), with this
   release's ServiceAccount as the one identity allowed to push:

     server:
       documents:
         source: operator
         operator:
           issuer: https://kubernetes.default.svc.cluster.local
           audience: kura
           subject: system:serviceaccount:{{ .Release.Namespace }}:{{ include "weebo-operator.serviceAccountName" . }}
     networkPolicy:
       operatorNamespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Name the server:

     kubectl apply -f - <<YAML
     apiVersion: kura.weebo.io/v1alpha1
     kind: KuraInstance
     metadata:
       name: kura
     spec:
       url: http://kura.kura.svc:8080
       auth:
         serviceAccountToken:
           audience: kura
     YAML

3. Apply your KuraTenant and KuraVault documents with kubectl; each shows
   Ready and a reason (`kubectl get kuravaults -A`).

`resource.customizations.health.kura.weebo.io_*`.
