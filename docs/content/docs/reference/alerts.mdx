title: Alerts
description: Every Prometheus alert the Helm chart installs, its expression, severity and what to do when it fires.
---

The Helm chart installs a PrometheusRule (when
`monitoring.prometheusRule.enabled`, the default) with the alerts of
[`deploy/helm/kura/files/alerts.yaml`](https://github.com/batleforc/weebo-kura/blob/main/deploy/helm/kura/files/alerts.yaml).
The backup alert of
[`files/backup-alerts.yaml`](https://github.com/batleforc/weebo-kura/blob/main/deploy/helm/kura/files/backup-alerts.yaml)
is added as its own group unless `backupsWaived` is `true`; if you waive
the chart's backups, alert on your own backups yourself. Each alert is
unit-tested with promtool (`task alerts:test`). Severities are `critical`,
`warning` and `info`. The metrics are described in
[Metrics](/docs/reference/metrics).

"For" is how long the expression must stay true before the alert fires.
The notification alerts never page (`warning`): notifications are off the
sync path.

### KuraUnready

```text
max(kura_ready) == 0
```

- **For:** 2m
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** No kura-server replica has a configuration loaded.
- **What to do:** Check the pods' logs. A replica with no stored configuration and invalid files stays unready; fix the documents (see `rejection` in `GET /v1/admin/status`) or the database connection.

### KuraDown

```text
absent(kura_ready)
```

- **For:** 5m
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** No kura-server replica is scraped.
- **What to do:** Check that the pods run and that Prometheus can reach the metrics port (ServiceMonitor, NetworkPolicy `networkPolicy.monitoringNamespace`).

### KuraDependencyFailing

```text
sum(rate(kura_unavailable_total[2m])) > 0
```

- **For:** 2m
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** Requests fail with 503 on a dependency (PostgreSQL, object storage, Vault or Authentik); the warn logs name it.
- **What to do:** Read the `warn` log lines of kura-server: they name the failing dependency. Fix PostgreSQL, object storage, Vault or Authentik.

### KuraVaultFailing

```text
sum(increase(kura_vault_call_duration_seconds_count{outcome="error"}[10m])) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** Vault Transit calls fail; vaults without a cached key answer 503.
- **What to do:** Check Vault (or OpenBao): sealed, unreachable, the Kubernetes auth role `kura`, or the tenant's Transit key `kura-<tenant>`.

### KuraNotifyLag

```text
max(kura_notify_lag_seconds) > 30
```

- **For:** 5m
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** Notifications reach replicas more than 30 s late.
- **What to do:** Look at PostgreSQL load and the network between replicas and the database. Devices still sync, but notifications arrive late.

### KuraNotifyQueueFilling

```text
max(kura_notify_queue_usage) > 0.5
```

- **For:** 1m
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** PostgreSQL's NOTIFY queue is over half full; commits fail when it is full.
- **What to do:** Look for a long-running transaction on the database: PostgreSQL cannot free `NOTIFY` queue entries older than it. End it before the queue fills and commits fail.

### KuraChunkCorrupt

```text
increase(kura_chunk_corrupt_total[10m]) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** A stored chunk failed its check on read.
- **What to do:** Look for the failing chunk in the logs, then check object storage. `kura-server check-chunks --sample <n>` decrypts random chunks and checks their address.

### KuraConfigRejected

```text
increase(kura_config_rejected_total[10m]) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** A configuration change was refused; the previous one stays in force.
- **What to do:** Read `rejection` in `GET /v1/admin/status` (file, document index, field) or the `warn` log, and fix the document.

### KuraDeletionScheduled

```text
max(kura_deletions_scheduled) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** A tenant or vault removed with Delete will be purged after its 30-day grace period.
- **What to do:** Confirm the removal was meant. To cancel it, put the document back before the 30 days end.

### KuraTenantAwaitingKeyDestruction

```text
max(kura_tenants_awaiting_key_destruction) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `info`
- **Summary:** A purged tenant's Transit key awaits an operator.
- **What to do:** After the tenant is purged, destroy its Transit key under the operator role in Vault; the tenant then leaves the gauge.

### KuraVaultEpochChanged

```text
increase(kura_vault_epoch_changes_total[15m]) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** Vault epochs changed (promotion or restore); every device resyncs.
- **What to do:** Expected after a failover, a switchover or a restore. Otherwise find out why the database timeline changed.

### KuraTenantQuotaHigh

```text
max by (tenant) (kura_quota_used_ratio) > 0.9
```

- **For:** 15m
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** `Tenant {{ $labels.tenant }} is above 90% of its quota.`
- **What to do:** Raise the tenant's quota, or ask the tenant to remove content. Past 100%, writes are refused.

### KuraStorageMetricsStale

```text
time() - max(kura_storage_refreshed_timestamp_seconds) > 600
```

- **For:** 5m
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** Storage and quota metrics have not been refreshed for 10 minutes; quota alerts act on stale data.
- **What to do:** Check the `storage metrics not refreshed` warn logs and the database.

### KuraIndexQueueGrowing

```text
min_over_time(deriv(max(kura_index_queue_depth)[15m:1m])[15m:1m]) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** The search index queue has grown for 15 minutes; search results lag behind edits.
- **What to do:** Check the replicas' load and the database; the indexer works through 100 notes at a time per replica.

### KuraIndexStalled

```text
time() - max(kura_index_round_timestamp_seconds) > 900
```

- **For:** 5m
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** The search indexer has not finished a round for 15 minutes; new edits are not searchable.
- **What to do:** Check the replicas' warn logs for indexer errors and the database.

### KuraIndexJobsFailed

```text
max(kura_index_failed) > 0
```

- **For:** 15m
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** `{{ $value }} notes could not be indexed after five attempts and are missing from search until edited; see the warn logs.`
- **What to do:** Read the warn logs for the failing notes. They come back into search at their next edit.

### KuraNotificationsStalled

```text
max(kura_notification_oldest_pending_seconds) > 900
```

- **For:** 10m
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** A notification delivery has waited more than 15 minutes; the worker is behind or stuck.
- **What to do:** Check the replicas' logs for the notification worker and the receivers' availability.

### KuraNotificationRuleDisabled

```text
max(kura_notification_rules_disabled) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** `{{ $value }} notification rules switched themselves off after 20 failed deliveries; a tenant admin re-enables them.`
- **What to do:** Fix the receiver, then a tenant admin re-enables the rule (a re-enabled rule forgets its failures).

### KuraGarbageCollectionStale

```text
(time() - max(kura_maintenance_gc_timestamp_seconds) > 172800) and max(kura_maintenance_gc_timestamp_seconds) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** Garbage collection has not run for 2 days (skipped while a backup holds its lock, or passes failing); storage keeps deleted content.
- **What to do:** Check that a backup is not stuck holding the backup lock, and read `kura_maintenance_failures_total` and the warn logs.

### KuraMaintenanceFailing

```text
increase(kura_maintenance_failures_total[2h]) > 0
```

- **For:** none (fires at once)
- **Severity:** `warning`
- **Summary:** Retention, garbage collection or a purge failed on a vault; see the warn logs.
- **What to do:** Read the warn logs: they name the vault. The other vaults went on.

### KuraBackupMissing

```text
time() - max(kura_backup_last_success_timestamp_seconds) > 36 * 3600
```

- **For:** 15m
- **Severity:** `critical`
- **Summary:** No bucket copy has completed for 36 hours (kura-server backup); restores would lose recent content.
- **What to do:** Check the `<release>-backup` CronJob and its logs, and the Secret naming the second bucket.
